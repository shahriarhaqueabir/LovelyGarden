# LG-010 Visual Plant Growth

## Goal

Make plant cards visually communicate growth and health.

## User Story

As a gardener, I want plant visuals to change as plants grow, struggle, or become harvest-ready.

## Scope

- Growth-stage icon/state.
- Stress and pest overlays.
- Harvest-ready visual treatment.
- Consistent legend.

## Definition of Done

- Visual state maps to lifecycle and health data.
- States are readable at small grid sizes.
- Legend or tooltips clarify symbols.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Given a plant advances stage, its grid card changes visual state.
- Given a plant has pests, its card shows pest state.
- Given a plant is harvest-ready, its card shows harvest affordance.
